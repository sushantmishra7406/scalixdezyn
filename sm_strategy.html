Common Strategy Types

Different brands need different social plays

Some brands need authority. Some need consistency. Some need conversion. A strong strategy chooses the right mix instead of trying to do everything at once.
01 Authority

Thought Leadership Strategy

Best for founders, agencies, consultants, and personal brands that need trust before they can win attention or close clients.

Educational content that proves expertise Opinion-led posts that shape brand perception Consistency designed to build top-of-mind authority
02 Community

Engagement Growth Strategy

Ideal when the goal is to increase reach, spark conversation, and make your audience interact with the brand more often.

Interactive content formats and strong hooks Series-based posting that encourages return visits Audience signals used to refine future content
03 Conversion

Lead Generation Strategy

Built for brands that want social media to move people toward inquiries, calls, bookings, or sales instead of only vanity metrics.

Offer-led content mapped to buyer intent Clear CTA structure across posts and stories Content connected to landing pages or DM flows
04 Launch

Campaign Push Strategy

Useful for new offers, launches, events, seasonal promotions, or short-term growth pushes that need stronger momentum in a fixed window.

Timed content phases from tease to conversion Creative repetition without feeling repetitive Performance tracking during the active campaign
